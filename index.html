How to join (1 minute per app)

Do this on your Android phone.

  1. Tap "Join test" on an app belowIt opens Google Play's tester page in Chrome. Check the account in the top-right corner is your tester Gmail.
  2. Tap "Become a tester"You'll see "You are now a tester". That's the important part.
  3. Tap "Install from Play Store"The Play Store app opens. Install it like any other app. If it says not available, wait 5–10 minutes and try again.
  4. Open the app once and look aroundA few taps is enough. Opening it every couple of days helps even more.
  5. Keep it installed for 14 daysPlease don't uninstall before 14 days. That's what counts for Google.
  6. Tick "I've joined" belowIt keeps track for you on this phone, so you know which ones are left.

Stuck? Quick fixes

The page says "App not available" or "Item not found"

You're signed in with a different Google account. In Chrome, tap your profile picture at the top of the Play page and switch to the Gmail you gave me. In the Play Store app, tap your profile picture and switch accounts there too. Then open the link again.

I don't see a "Become a tester" button

Your email may not be on my tester list yet. Send me the Gmail you want to use and I'll add it within a few minutes.

"Install" doesn't show after I became a tester

Google can take 5–10 minutes (sometimes a few hours) to activate it. Close the Play Store, come back later and tap "Install from Play Store" again.

I have an iPhone

These are Android apps, so testing only works on an Android phone. If you have an Android tablet or a second phone, that works too.

How do I send feedback?

On the app's Play Store page, scroll down to "Send private feedback to the developer". Or just message me directly. Bugs, ideas, anything helps.

Will it cost anything or collect my data?

No. All apps are free to test and nothing is charged. They're study and practice apps plus one party game.
